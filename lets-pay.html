Project overview

Let's Pay is a mobile first, web-based payment platform that enables Nigerians to send, receive, and manage money instantly using just a phone number, QR code, or bank transfer.

It is designed to remove the friction of failed transfers, multiple banking apps, and complex payment processes, giving individuals and small businesses a fast, secure, and accessible way to move money anytime, anywhere.

The problem

Nigerians struggle with failed transfers, multiple banking apps, and unreliable ways to send and receive money.

Our solution

Let's Pay provides a simple, web-based wallet that lets Nigerians send and receive money instantly using phone numbers, QR codes, and bank transfers.
